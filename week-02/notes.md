## In My Own Words

**What is the cybersecurity landscape, and why does it matter?**

```
The cybersecurity landscape is the entire environment that includes all systems, devices, software, users, attackers, and defenders, and it matters because this connected ecosystem creates many potential entry points that must be protected to keep the whole infrastructure secure
```

**How do hardware, OS, and software layers interact?**

```
The hardware, OS, and software work together as a chain. The software we use sends requests to the OS, and the OS manages the hardware to carry those requests out. Each layer depends on each other, so nothing works unless all three are communicating smoothly.
```
